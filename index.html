SIH 2026 · Occupational Heat-Safety Prototype

Protecting outdoor workers before heat becomes dangerous.

HeatSafe demonstrates a personalized heat-stress assessment system that combines environmental conditions, work intensity, exposure duration, and hydration status to provide an explainable, worker-level safety recommendation.
